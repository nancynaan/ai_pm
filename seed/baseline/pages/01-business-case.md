## Opportunity

Endless runners remain one of the most played casual genres on mobile. Sessions are short, the game is easy to learn and it monetizes well through a mix of in-app purchases and rewarded ads. {{value:client}} wants a polished, performance-friendly runner with a strong visual identity to grow its casual portfolio.

## Options considered

- **Do nothing:** no new revenue; the soft-launch learnings are lost.
- **Licensed runner template:** faster start, but weak differentiation and a revenue share with the template owner.
- **Own game (chosen):** full control of the economy and live-ops, higher upfront cost, best long-term margin.
